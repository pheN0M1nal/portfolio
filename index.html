Airwallex · Kafka · Datadog
integrationarchitecture

Airwallex integration and automated reconciliation

Problem
Reconciliation was done entirely by hand, and payment processing was tangled into invoice and order code inside the monolith. It was hard to monitor and hard to scale.
What I built
  • Integrated Airwallex as a payment provider and built automatic matching between payments and invoices.
  • Moved payment processing out of the monolith into its own service, with Kafka events between the payment, invoice and order flows.
  • Generated Airwallex payment links automatically when an order is created, and published events for downstream services.
  • Extended the service to Airwallex Global Accounts and multiple Airwallex entities.
  • Added Datadog monitoring with Slack alerts for the payment service.

ResultThe manual reconciliation process was retired, and payments became a service the team could watch and scale on its own.

Node.js · PostgreSQL
reconciliationedge cases

Reconciliation V2: one payment, many invoices

Problem
Customers often paid several invoices in one transfer into a global account. Combined with partial payments and inconsistent callbacks, these still needed a person to sort out.
What I built
  • A second version of the engine that settles a single payment across multiple invoices, by checking due invoices against settled amounts.
  • Handling for more callback scenarios and payment edge cases.
  • A manual reconciliation dashboard for anything the engine can't settle.
  • Alerts to the accounts receivable team, so unresolved payments get flagged.

Result70–80% of reconciliation now runs automatically. The rest reaches a person with context.

Stripe expansion to the UAE and Saudi Arabia

Problem
Stripe was live in the US, UK and EU. Launching in two new regions meant supporting local payment methods and being ready for a jump in volume on day one.
What I built
  • Region-specific payment methods on top of the existing Stripe integration.
  • Capacity work ahead of launch for the expected payment volume.
  • Datadog monitoring and Slack alerts so failed payments surfaced within minutes.

ResultThe two regions added hundreds of payments a day.

NetSuite
accountingdata integrity

NetSuite accounting sync

Problem
Every payment had to reach the accounting system accurately, and our own records had to agree with it.
What I built
  • A sync that, for each payment, creates it in NetSuite, confirms the invoice exists there, marks the invoice paid, and only then updates our database.
  • Notifications on every success and failure, so finance could see where each payment stood.

ResultPayments reach accounting through one checked path, and failures are visible instead of silent.

Alongside these I handled production payment support: investigating why individual Stripe and Airwallex payments failed and explaining the cause to customers, plus performance fixes and features across the main platform.
